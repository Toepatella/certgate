## Bottom line

Across six lanes and roughly 60 candidate papers, nothing collides with CertGate's headline: no located work makes the site the unit of independence for a finite-sample certificate on a selective gate's answered-set error, and the closest objects — SCoRE's record-level e-value selective risk control, Lee & Ren's hierarchical conformal e-values for FDR-controlled selection, Zhou & Wang's diagnosis that record-level WSR certificates overrun under grouped deployment, and the Dunn / Lee-Barber-Willett cluster-exchangeability machinery — each stop one axis short, exactly as the manuscript's related-work framing says. The two secondary contributions are near-misses on their components rather than their combinations: the label-shift mode has uncited pairing precedents (Plassier 2023 for multi-site label-shift guarantees; Alexandari 2018 for abstention under label shift), and the abstention-explanation layer has an uncited direct precedent for explaining a linear model's rejections (Fernandes & Rocha 2026); both contributions are already framed modestly in the draft, so the fix is citation and one positioning clause each, not a re-scoping. The validation-design and eICU claims stand. Eight additions to `references.bib` close the gaps a reviewer would raise; the manuscript's existing 39-entry list already carries every other paper the lanes flagged as reviewer-expected.
