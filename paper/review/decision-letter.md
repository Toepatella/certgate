# Decision: **Major revision**

*Discover Computing* · Collection: "Intelligent Medicine: Machine Learning and Explainable AI for Next-Generation Healthcare"
Manuscript: *CertGate: finite-sample certified selective prediction for multi-site clinical risk models, with label-shift robustness and explainable abstention*
Handling editor · six reports received, all six recommending major revision · editorial office audit complete

---

Dear Authors,

I am able to offer a major revision. I want to be direct about what that means here, because the volume is unusual: the office audit put 361 raised findings through verification and 282 survived — 267 confirmed, 15 plausible, 77 merged as co-discovery, two killed outright (DS-06, R2-41) and one narrowed (DS-07). A paper drawing 282 surviving findings from six referees is normally a reject. This one is not, for two reasons, and I want both on the record before the criticism.

First, the arithmetic is clean. Referee 5's audit reconciled every number in the paper — Table 1's bins recover the stated 0.05 exceedance exactly, all four published Clopper–Pearson intervals recompute, all three Table 3 fractions recompute from their counts, the bibliography has zero dangling keys and zero orphaned entries — and Referee 4, with repository access, independently re-verified the test suite, the intervals, byte-identical regeneration of E5/E6 including PNG hashes, and every quantitative claim it could trace. Nothing was drifted or fabricated. Second, a scan for novelty inflation returns zero hits; every "first" in the manuscript is ordinal. The disclosure apparatus Referee 2 credits — the five-clause guarantee, the two-number violation protocol, the enforced negative-control abort, the six-item limitations list — is real and is unusual. Almost every revision I am about to require is *additive*. That is why this is a revision and not a rejection.

But I must be equally direct that the paper as submitted does not yet belong in this Collection, and the reason is specific to this Collection rather than generic.

## Why this Collection, specifically

This Collection's central emphasis is explainability — framed both as a transparency requirement and as an educational aid for clinicians. Your title, your abstract, one of your four keywords, and your third contribution all promise it. The paper then demotes it twice in its own voice: §3.8 calls the explanation layer "a supporting capability the linear head makes nearly free," and §1 contribution 3 says it "supports the certificate above rather than standing as an independent method." Five referees reached the same place independently from four different angles. Referee 5 and Referee 1 both established that explainable abstention is one of the four literatures §2 sets up and is dropped from *both* of your own three-literature framings (§1 and §6). Referee 3 read all six items in §6.1 and found every one statistical and none about explanation. Referee 2 found "fairness", "equity", "protected", "demograph" and "subgroup" matching a single line in the whole manuscript — while `ifac2025abstainexplain`, whose title is "Interpretable and **Fair** Mechanisms for Abstaining Classifiers", is cited five times and never once for its fairness content. Referee 3 found zero occurrences of "faithful", "plausib", "user study", "human evaluation", "education", "human-cent" or "workflow", and no feature in the paper carries a clinical name — features 0–7 are integer indices.

And then §2.4 closes by volunteering that "the same certificate shape appears in power-grid contingency screening, so it is not specific to medicine." Referee 1 and Referee 5 both flagged that sentence. In a Collection scoped to clinical, epidemiological and public-health ML, a sentence disclaiming medical specificity — sourced to a single unrefereed cross-domain preprint — spends credibility for nothing. Delete it.

The result is a paper whose statistical contribution is substantial and whose explainability contribution, as evidenced, is one methods subsection, one experiment resting on **two declined cases**, and one exactness claim that all six referees independently established is wrong as written. That is not a fit for this Collection. It is repairable, and Section 3 below says how — but it is the thing that determines whether I can accept a revision.

Manuscript mechanics I will note once and not again: the submission contains six figure captions and no figures. Five referees reported it independently (Referees 1, 2, 3, 5 and the desk screen). No image markup, no `.png`/`.pdf`/`.svg` reference, no in-text callout to any figure anywhere in §1–§6 or the appendices. Referee 4, who could open the files in the repository, found three of them defective in ways you would have seen had they been in the manuscript: `E3_concept_shift.png` renders its title truncated mid-word as "…negative control (certificate shou", with the axes occupying half the canvas and the "no certificates" annotation stranded in the blank margin. A submission in this state cannot be sent to referees a second time.

## The referees, and where they disagree

**Referee 1 (distribution-free inference) and Referee 5 (forensic) independently identify the paper's central statistical gap**, and this is the finding that carries the most weight in my decision. R_M is defined in §3.3 as a ratio of sums over sites; §3.1 and §3.7(1) claim it holds "at a new target site". Neither §3, nor A.1, nor A.2 contains a transport step from the one to the other. A.1(ii) reinforces the population reading — "E[R_M] is a ratio of expectations" — and §3.3's "Three scope facts follow directly — per-target-site scope…" asserts the conclusion without deriving it. Two referees working from different premises landed on the same sentence, and the desk screen independently supplied the remedy question. The office confirms it: the claim as written is not derivable from what you prove.

**The same two referees independently identify the second gap, in near-identical terms.** Under A.1(ii)'s design-conditioning the atoms have site-specific means; A.1(iii)'s per-step inequality then evaluates to 1 + λ_t(α − μ_t), which exceeds 1 at any site with μ_t < α even when the average satisfies H₀. The supermartingale property fails atom-wise, before any product is formed. You state no i.i.d.-or-exchangeable assumption on the calibration sites anywhere, and §6.1 then concedes exactly the dependence structure that would break it. The office corrected Referee 1 here in a way that *strengthens* him: his illustrative bets are not producible by your own λ schedule, but a realizable pair (1.0, 0.5) with atom means straddling α gives E[K₂] = 1.125 > 1. Take the corrected version.

**All six referees found the exact-Shapley claim.** This is the most heavily corroborated finding in the pool. §3.8 states, unqualified, "For a linear model these attributions are exact Shapley values, with no approximation or sampling," and §4.6 repeats "genuine Shapley values, not sampled approximations." The identity φ_j = w_j(x_j − E[x_j]) holds under feature independence or under the interventional value function; you name neither, and §4.1's generator — class means separated along a single shared direction supported on coordinates 0–3 — induces marginal correlation among precisely the features being explained. Referee 4 confirmed it from `data.py`. Referee 3 computed the magnitude at ≈0.09, which is real but modest, and I ask you to fix this honestly rather than dramatically: name the value function, state the condition, and if you invoke the interventional function say plainly that the attributions then answer a different question. The word "exact" appears attached to attributions nine times in this manuscript. Every one is currently unearned.

**Referee 3 and Referee 4 converge, from opposite directions, on the evidence for your third contribution.** §4.6 reports a three-decimal cohort statistic (1.722), an eight-feature gap ranking, a gap of −0.854 and the adverb "systematically" — over **two declined cases**, at τ* = 0.55, which Referee 4 confirmed from `constants.py` is the exact minimum of your own 23-point grid, i.e. the operating point at which abstention is rarest. Referee 3 supplies the mechanism argument I find decisive: you report features 0–3 at 1.157, 1.161, 1.178 and 1.155 — a spread of 0.023 across four coefficients on a single shared signal direction — so there is no stated mechanism by which feature 0 should differ from 1–3 in abstention behaviour. Referee 3 further observes that a case is declined precisely when signed contributions *cancel*, so a large mean |φ_j| on declined cases identifies a feature that had to be offset, not one that caused the abstention. The causal word "driver" is not licensed by the statistic you report. Ten findings across five referees flag this. It is the single most replicated defect in the pool and it sits in the section this Collection's editors will read most closely.

**Referee 4 is the only referee with repository access, and it shows.** Forty-three of its forty-seven findings survived contact with the artifact. The one I want you to answer first: `run_synthetic.py:40` sets `SHIFT_SEP = 1.8`, consumed only by E2 and E3 — the two experiments producing your 48.5% and 83% headlines — against §4.1's single stated `sep = 2.2`, which is what E1, E4, E5 and E6 use. The word `sep` occurs once in your entire manuscript. The code comment reads *"realistic head so shift bites."* And `tests/test_constants.py` does not pin it, alongside `ANCHOR_SITES`, `SHIFT_BASE`, `CONCEPT_INTERCEPT` and every `SimConfig` default. Your §3.2 says the constants test "removes the degrees of freedom that would otherwise let a tunable pipeline flatter itself." I do not believe there was bad faith here and no referee alleged any. But this is the exact shape of a researcher degree of freedom, sitting outside the mechanism you offer as protection against researcher degrees of freedom, in the two experiments that produce your two most-quoted numbers. **Disclosure plus a sensitivity table at sep = 2.2 is a condition of acceptance.**

**Where referees disagree, and how I resolve it.**

*Referee 4 versus Referees 2, 3 and 5 on venue fit.* Referee 4 offered the view that the work sits comfortably on this Collection's encouraged list and that no reservation requires new experiments. Referees 2, 3, 5 and the desk screen reached the opposite conclusion. The office downgraded Referee 4's view to plausible and gave the reason I adopt: venue fit is not a reproducibility referee's remit, Referee 4's anchors do not address the two demotion passages, and Referee 4's own R4-46 asks for E5 to be re-run at a different operating point — which is a new experiment. **Resolved for the majority.** Referee 4's positive verification findings stand and are recorded; its fit clearance does not.

*The desk screen versus Referee 5 on the truncation counterexample.* The desk screen rated "provably anti-conservative" a major defect; Referee 5 rated it minor. The office kept Referee 5's sharper two-quote anchor and flagged that the desk screen's severity is the better call. **I agree with the desk screen.** §3.3 says "provably", forwards to A.3, and A.3 restates the headline numbers and forwards to a regression test. No site configuration, no error rates, no argument appears anywhere — and Appendix A is titled *Deferred proofs* while A.3 is *Software and reproducibility details*. That construction is the sole justification for your influence-weighting estimand. It is major.

*Referee 4 versus Referees 1, 2, 3, 5 and the desk screen on the figures.* Not a disagreement, though it reads as one. Referee 4 wrote of "the six embedded figures" because it was looking at the PNGs in the repository; the other five were looking at the manuscript, which embeds none. Both are true and both must be addressed.

**Where the office corrected or killed a referee's point, so you are not asked to answer something that is not there.**

- **DS-06 is killed.** The claim that the manuscript has no reference list resolving to anything a referee can inspect is refuted by the desk screen's own conduct — four of its findings cite `references.bib` by line number. Do not respond to it. The residual production reminder is folded into the availability item.
- **R2-41 is killed.** The claim that A.3 gives a bare test count with no description of what the suite verifies is false: you describe four specific tests, one with its full numerical construction. The narrow and sound version of the point survives as DS-28 — the integer 69 is not itself assessable — and that is what I am asking you to fix.
- **DS-07 survives narrowed.** Your `[TO BE COMPLETED]` Funding, Author contributions and Competing interests fields are deliberate placeholders and are not a defect. What survives is the Data availability statement's placeholder repository URL and the absence of any Code availability section.
- **Several clauses are struck and you should not be asked to defend them.** The claim that the 0/9 rate is "the only rate reported without a Clopper–Pearson interval" is false — intervals appear on four numbers and dozens are bare, and the desk screen contradicts itself on this. DS-19's "no evidence that the BBSE mode ever functions as a correction" overstates: nine draws certified with zero hard-violations is weak but non-zero evidence. R1-30's complaint that the declined count is never stated is defeated by its own anchor — you do say "declining 2".

## What must change

Not open to further argument. The revision plan (`revision-plan.md`) carries all 282 findings by ID with what, where, and what closes each; these are the items on which I will not accept a rebuttal in place of a change.

1. **Restate the certified estimand to match what you prove.** R_M is a cross-site influence-weighted parameter. Either supply the transport step to a single new site, or restate §3.1 and §3.7(1) as a statement about the site-population mean and say plainly in §5.1 what shrinks. I expect the second. Do not argue that §3.3's "three scope facts follow directly" already does this; the office checked, and it does not.

2. **State the sampling assumption on calibration sites as a numbered assumption, and repair A.1(iii).** The supermartingale does not follow from H₀ as currently stated. If §6.1's conceded temporal correlation is real, quantify the level inflation or convert the concession into an explicit assumption with a sensitivity note.

3. **Withdraw or condition the exact-Shapley claim, in all nine places.** Name the value function. Report the empirical correlation among features 0–3. Define μ_j and "base".

4. **Replace the n=2 abstention-driver result.** Re-run E5 at a certified operating point with a material decline rate, replicate it, report per-case signed attributions, and state the denominator in the Figure 5 caption. If the effect does not survive replication, say so and delete the causal language. A negative result here is publishable; the current result is not.

5. **Disclose `sep = 1.8` and report E2/E3 at 2.2.** Non-negotiable, per above.

6. **Supply the figures, fixed.** Six figures embedded, in-text callouts, captions that describe panel content rather than restating body numbers, denominators in captions that travel alone, and the three rendering defects Referee 4 documented repaired.

7. **Deposit the code and data at a DOI-minting archive, with a licence, and add a Code availability section.** Springer requires it and eleven of your specific reproducibility claims currently terminate at "[CODE REPOSITORY URL — to be added]".

8. **Report the 0/9 interval and the BBSE diagnostics.** Your abstract's second headline sentence ("the corrected mode never does") rests on nine draws with an exact upper bound of 0.3363 — 6.7× your δ. Referee 4 established that `fit_bbse` computes ρ̂, the ρ interval, `gap_lo`, `n_boot` and `n_attempts` into `BBSEFit.diagnostics` and that `run_synthetic.py` writes none of them, and that all 191 declines carry the generic `failsafe` reason while the three named BBSE declines fire zero times in any reported experiment. As released, E2 cannot distinguish a working correction from an underpowered one. Write the diagnostics out, sweep the shift magnitude, and report BBSE's behaviour under no shift.

9. **Earn the explainability contribution or retitle the paper.** This is the Collection-fit condition. Concretely: a faithfulness or fidelity assessment against a comparator explanation method; clinically named features in at least one worked vignette with an EHR-facing abstention message; an explanation-facing item in §6.1; explainable abstention restored to your own literature counts in §1 and §6; engagement with the fairness content of the abstaining-classifier work you already cite; and either a defence of the demotion sentences or their removal. If you do not want to do this, the honest alternative is to retitle and resubmit as a selective-prediction methods paper — but then it is not a paper for this Collection.

10. **Rewrite the abstract.** It carries three undefined terms of art, uses "budget" for both α and δ within one paragraph, omits "influence-weighted" from the estimand, omits the asymptotic caveat your §3.6 claims "every guarantee statement carries", and states its two strongest claims on its two thinnest bases. The office's framing is correct and I adopt it.

11. **Delete the missingness-encoder sentence in §6.1.** Referee 5 found it from the manuscript alone (a component described nowhere in Methods, inapplicable to a generator producing complete features); Referee 4 found from the code that the actual behaviour is the *inverse* — non-finite features raise. Two referees from opposite directions on a fictional component, in the section your paper most relies on for credibility.

12. **Delete "published" as a descriptor for `zhou2026falsesense`** (§1 contribution 4, §4.4) and attribute all seven unrefereed arXiv `@misc` entries as preprints in text. Seven of your thirty-one references carry the motivating 9–30% figure, the closest-competitor identity on which contribution 1's entire novelty delta rests, and your federated positioning.

13. **Repair the real-data justification.** §5.5's "Real data cannot supply that ground truth, which is what makes it unable to validate a validity claim" is refuted by your own §3.9: the hard-violation criterion consumes realized answered errors and a Wilson bound, both computable from a labelled retrospective multi-site cohort. Four referees found this. It is the load-bearing defence for your entire evidence base and it does not hold.

14. **Add one comparator.** Contribution 1's necessity currently rests on a citation. Run a record-as-unit certificate on the same E1 draws under the same screen and report its violation rate. Referee 1's question form specifies exactly this and it is, by my reading, the cheapest high-value experiment in the whole plan.

## What I consider settled

**Settled in your favour, and not to be sanded away.** The office is emphatic on this and I endorse it. Your arithmetic audit is clean. Your register is restrained — zero hits for "novel", "unprecedented", "state-of-the-art", "for the first time", "paradigm", "breakthrough". Your §3.7 and §3.9 scoping clauses are stronger than typical for this venue. §4.5 volunteers a cross-run seed difference no referee would have caught. Your concept-shift boundary is correctly framed and no referee attacked it as a weakness. **A revision that replaces hedges with confidence would be a worse paper and I would not accept it.** Almost every item above asks you to add evidence, define a symbol, or delete an overstatement — not to assert more.

The register findings you will see in the plan (twelve instances of the disclosure motif; three of anticipatory pre-emption at an imagined critic) ask you to *stop saying* you are being honest and let the apparatus say it. They do not ask you to be less honest.

**Settled against you, and not open to rebuttal.** The estimand mismatch is not repairable by rewording — either the derivation appears or the claim narrows. The exact-Shapley claim is wrong as stated; six referees is not a reading disagreement. The n=2 result does not support "systematically" or "driver" at any level of disclosure. `sep = 1.8` must be disclosed. The figures must be present. The code must be deposited. I will not litigate these.
